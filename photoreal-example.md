# Example: Photoreal Specification Lock and Prompt

**Request:** "A samurai resting under a cherry blossom tree at sunset, realistic."

## Specification Lock
- **Realism level:** photoreal (user said "realistic"). Not stylized, not fantasy art.
- **Hero subject:** one adult male in historically plausible lacquered armor, seated against a rock, eyes closed, relaxed pose. Katana in its scabbard resting beside him.
- **Supporting elements (max 3):** one blossoming cherry tree overhead, a rocky hillside with moss, a distant hazy mountain ridge. No pagoda, no waterfalls, no extra landmarks.
- **Time and light:** low sun behind and to the right, warm golden-hour backlight with soft fill from the sky, natural rim light on the armor.
- **Camera:** full-frame camera, 85mm lens, f/2.8, shallow natural depth of field, eye-level framing, 4:5 portrait.
- **Texture and realism:** real skin pores and light stubble, flyaway hair, worn and slightly scuffed lacquer, fabric creases, moss and rock texture, a few scattered petals on the ground only.
- **Resolution target:** highest native size supported, then real upscale to at least 2048 px on the long edge.
- **Avoid:** HDR look, oversaturation, glowing fantasy sky, plastic skin, painterly or CGI look, floating petal clutter, extra fingers, stretched limbs, warped sword, impossible armor.

## Prompt
Photorealistic photograph, full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/2.8, a middle-aged Japanese man in worn, historically accurate lacquered samurai armor sitting relaxed against a mossy rock beneath a blossoming cherry tree, eyes closed, face turned slightly toward the setting sun, a katana in its scabbard leaning against the rock beside him. Low golden-hour sun behind him casting warm rim light on the armor edges, soft sky fill on his face, natural color grading with realistic saturation, visible skin pores and light stubble, scuffed lacquer and creased fabric, a few petals resting on the stones, distant hazy mountain ridge softly out of focus. Natural scale and proportions, correct hands and anatomy, no HDR look, no painterly texture, no oversaturation, no extra decoration.

## What the report must then confirm
Declared level: photoreal. Real output size, whether upscaled and with what tool, results of the 100% zoom check on face, hands, armor, and sword, the realism and photoreal inspection results, and the final prompt used.
