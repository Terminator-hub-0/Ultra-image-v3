# Mandatory Video Workflow

1. Extract requirements, platform, duration, resolution, frame rate, and audio/voice needs.
2. Perform feasibility, risk, and format analysis.
3. Define story arc and shot list.
4. Lock identity, wardrobe, props, environment, and style.
5. Plan camera, lighting, movement, and transitions.
6. Define the voice spec and exact script; plan graphics, titles, and captions.
7. Complete the Specification Lock for every shot.
8. Generate reference frames when supported.
9. Select text-to-video, image-to-video, or video-to-video.
10. Generate shots/clips and wait for completion.
11. Inspect actual video or frames: first, last, midpoint, every cut, transition, and graphic.
12. Evaluate story, continuity, motion, physics, camera, graphics, and technical quality.
13. Generate realistic voice, music, and effects; check pronunciation, lip-sync, levels, and loudness.
14. Correct defective shots only; do not deliver known fixable defects.
15. Assemble/export when supported at the highest supported resolution.
16. Verify final playback and measure duration, resolution, frame rate, codec, and audio.
17. Deliver with the mandatory Video Production Report, including Generation Method & Provenance.
