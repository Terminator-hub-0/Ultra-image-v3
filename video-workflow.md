# Mandatory Video Workflow

1. Extract requirements, platform, duration, and audio needs.
2. Perform feasibility, risk, and format analysis.
3. Define story arc and shot list.
4. Lock identity, wardrobe, props, environment, and style.
5. Plan camera, lighting, movement, and transitions.
6. Generate reference frames when supported.
7. Select text-to-video, image-to-video, or video-to-video.
8. Generate shots/clips and wait for completion.
9. Inspect actual video or frames.
10. Evaluate story, continuity, motion, physics, camera, audio, and export.
11. Correct defective shots only.
12. Assemble/export when supported.
13. Verify final playback and technical properties.
14. Deliver with the mandatory Video Production Report.
